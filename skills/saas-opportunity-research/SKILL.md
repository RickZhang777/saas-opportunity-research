# SaaS Opportunity Research

[English](#start-en) · [简体中文](#start-zh)

<a id="start-en"></a>
## English

Use the requested language or the current conversation language. Start by recovering the target market, operator resources, product scope, available evidence, and any earlier decisions. State a working assumption for a missing preference if it can be reversed cheaply.

- **Scout** for new opportunities. Expand the pool, deduplicate, screen for a specific buyer and deliverable, and investigate only the promising candidates.
- **Investigate** a named candidate. Compare how buyers solve the same job today, including free or manual alternatives. Find the strongest reason the opportunity might fail.
- **Review** a portfolio. Reuse dated evidence, update the gaps that can change the decision, and group opportunities by buyer, trigger, and accepted result.

Lead with **validate first**, **watch**, or **defer**. Give up to two pieces of evidence that affect the decision, the main uncertainty, and one inexpensive validation action. Zero recommendations is a valid result.

Keep competitor evidence, evidence from existing alternatives, and evidence for the proposed offer separate. Attach sources, dates, and limits to material claims. Two nonzero comparable monthly observations give one change; three increasing months give two. Track visits, users, revenue, MRR, and profit separately. A price page, rising traffic, or a competitor's payments cannot validate the proposed offer.

The default endpoint is a recommendation and a validation plan. Publishing, development, spending, and outreach follow the user's authorization. When the user asks for an approved implementation handoff, include the buyer, evidence, scope, acceptance criteria, and remaining unknowns.

## 简体中文

按用户指定语言或当前对话语言回答。先恢复目标市场、经营资源、产品范围、已有证据和用户决定。缺少偏好时，可对容易调整的事项注明工作假设。

- **发现机会**：扩大候选池、去重，筛选买家和交付明确的产品，对较有价值的候选深入核验。
- **深入调研**：对指定产品，比较客户现在怎样完成同一个任务，含免费及人工替代。找最强反证。
- **复盘候选库**：沿用注明日期的证据，补会改变判断的缺口，按买家、触发时刻和可验收结果归并方向。

开头给出**优先验证、观察或暂缓**，紧跟一至两条影响决策的证据、最大未知和一个低成本验证动作。合格推荐可以为零。

分别记录竞品证据、现有替代证据和自己方案的证据。关键结论带来源、日期和局限。两个非零且可比较的月份只代表一次变化，三个月依次上涨才代表两次上涨。访问量、用户数、营收、MRR 与利润分开。价格页、流量上涨和竞品收款不能验证自己的报价。

默认交付建议和验证方案。发布、开发、付款、外联按用户授权执行。用户要求把已确认的方向交付实施时，写明买家、证据、首版范围、验收条件和仍待核项。
